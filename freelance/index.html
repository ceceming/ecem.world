Payment Terms

Deposit
50% upfront to book the project; 50% on final delivery or launch. Projects over $3,000: 40% deposit, 30% at design approval, 30% at launch.
Monthly plans
Billed on the 1st of each month, in advance. Minimum 3 months; cancel with 30 days' notice.
Due date
Invoices are due within 14 days.
Late payments
1.5% per month on overdue balances; work pauses after 14 days overdue.
Payment methods
Interac e-Transfer or bank transfer; credit card via Stripe (+3% processing).

Terms

Revisions
2 rounds included per deliverable. Extra rounds are billed at $48/h.
Third-party costs
Shopify plans, domains, hosting, paid apps, fonts, stock images and printing are paid by the client directly.
Ownership
Full rights to final deliverables transfer to the client once paid in full. Unused concepts and working files stay with the designer.
Portfolio use
The designer may show the finished work in her portfolio and on social media unless agreed otherwise in writing.
Client responsibilities
Provide content, brand assets, logins and feedback on time. Delays over 2 weeks may move the project to the next open slot.
Out of scope
Anything not listed is quoted separately or billed at $48/h, with approval first.
Cancellation
The deposit is non-refundable. If the client cancels mid-project, completed work is billed at the hourly rate.
Substack content
Posts are written by the designer in the client's voice and approved by the client before publishing. Growth results are not guaranteed.
