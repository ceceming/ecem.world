Who's behind it

Hi, I'm Ecem. One designer, from the first sketch to the box at your door.

I'm a designer based in Toronto. Most merch companies hand you a catalogue and a spot for your logo. I start from your brand and design a small collection that belongs to it, with the same care I'd give an identity project.

Then I source it. I work directly with manufacturers in China and act as your bridge to them, which is how the prices stay fair. I check the quality before anything ships, and I deliver it to your door, or to each person on your team.

You'll talk to me at every step. There is no account manager in between.
